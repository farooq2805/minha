Minha Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Privacy-First Architecture

Your privacy and trust are paramount. Minha ("we", "our", or "the app") is designed from the ground up to respect your spiritual journey. We believe your Quran reading, prayer tracking, and supplications should remain private to you.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Minha operates with a local-first architecture. The following data is stored exclusively on your device using encrypted local preferences:

  • Prayer Tracker History: Which daily prayers you have checked off.
  • Quran Reading Progress: Your last-read verse, bookmark positions, and juz completion.
  • Fasting Log: The days of Ramadan you have marked as fasted.
  • Bookmarked Verses: Verses you have saved to your personal reflection collection.
  • Display Name: Your chosen name for cards and greetings.

This data does not leave your phone and is not sold, rented, or transferred to any third party.

2. Location & Prayer Calculation

To accurately calculate Islamic prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha), Minha calculates astronomical positions directly on your phone using latitude and longitude coordinates. Your location is never logged, stored on an external server, or tracked.

3. AI Companion ("Ask Minha")

Minha includes an optional conversational Islamic companion powered by Google's Gemini API:

  • When you ask a question in "Ask Minha", the text of your query is transmitted over an encrypted HTTPS connection to Google Generative AI to generate relevant Quranic and Hadith citations.
  • Queries do not contain your personal identifying information.
  • Google may process query prompts in accordance with the Google Gemini API Terms of Service.
  • You may provide your own free Google Gemini API key in settings at any time.

4. Advertising & Monetization

  • Zero ads on Quran verses: We never display advertisements, popups, or commercial interruptions while you are reading or reciting the Holy Quran.
  • Rewarded video ads may be optionally watched to unlock additional daily questions or bonus features. Ad network partners may receive standard non-personal device identifiers for ad delivery.

5. Security of Your Data

All communication with external Islamic APIs (such as text, word-by-word meaning from Quran.com, and audio recitation streams) is conducted over standard secure TLS/HTTPS protocols.
